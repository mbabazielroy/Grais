How it works

1) Prediction. We estimate shortage probability, severity, and timing per region using a calibrated ML model plus optional time-series (if enabled). Inputs include population, supply, consumption, and risk factors.

2) Optimization. We solve a multi-resource LP to allocate shipments from depots to regions under constraints (distance, budget, vehicle capacity, cold chain). Strict mode penalizes unmet demand more heavily.

3) Prioritization. We rank regions by combining probability, severity, urgency, unmet demand, and allocation bonuses, with heuristic or LLM reasoning (if configured).

4) Scenarios. You can apply outages, demand surges, and budget caps. Use presets or custom inputs, and compare against baseline.

5) Data sources. Choose synthetic, real sample, or remote feeds. Warnings surface if data is stale, degraded, or falls back to synthetic.
